$ cat 2006-2013.log

Madrid. Telecommunication engineering.

Technical University of Madrid, 2006 to 2013. Signals, networks, and the first programs that did what I meant instead of what I typed.

2010 to 2011: Erasmus year at Metropolia University of Applied Sciences, Helsinki.

$ cat 2014-2017.log

Bolsas y Mercados Españoles. Full-stack developer.

The Spanish stock exchange. A B2B electronic trading platform: institutional brokers, market access over FIX.

Technical support for the people whose trading day depended on it. Optimised transaction processing: 20% less processing time and a more stable platform.

$ cat 2017-2021.log

Department of Education. Technical college lead teacher.

2016 to 2017: Master's in Teacher Training, URJC Madrid. Thesis: programming education through video games.

Then four years teaching in technical colleges in Madrid and Valencia. Four schools, 60 to 120 students a year. OOP, SQL, networks, web programming, systems administration.

Lead teacher meant coordinating the teaching team of each school: every subject aligned to the official programme, every teacher on the agreed syllabus and pace, every student's objectives tracked across subjects.

This is where "I make hard stacks teachable" was earned.

$ cat 2022-2023.log

Dapper Labs. Smart contract engineer on Flow.

Authored the Fungible Token Switchboard: one receiver capability for any token. Contract, tests, docs and follow-ups. Part of the Flow FT standard, deployed on mainnet.

Authored the Fungible Tokens Metadata FLIP, merged August 2022, and the FungibleTokenMetadataViews contract.

Hybrid custody contracts for walletless onboarding. Six documentation PRs to the Cadence language. Drafted the Cadence Style Guide FLIP. Led the FLIP discussions for the token standards. Flow Certified Developer.

$ cat 2023-2025.log

Freelance. Smart contract engineer.

Ported the Band Protocol oracle from Solidity to Cadence: testnet October 2023, mainnet-ready January 2024, Cadence 1.0 August 2024.

Upgraded Celer cBridge and its auxiliary contracts to Cadence 1.0. The suite was audited by Oak Security.

Built Trade Wars: a fully on-chain, massively multiplayer trading game on Sui. Move and TypeScript.

Documentation upstream: cadence-lang.org, cadence-tools, aptos-core.

$ cat 2025-now.log

Sui Foundation. Solutions engineer.

Both halves of the story become one job.

Sui Builders Bootcamp: five days of Move and Sui plus a hackathon day, for cohorts of 30 to 40 pre-selected developers. Ankara, Bucharest, Paris, and online. At every stop, the local ambassadors are trained to run the next edition on their own.

Hackathons: Sui's presence at ETHGlobal New York and Lisbon (800+ hackers each), EncodeHub London, and online hackathons of 100+ developers. Define the workshop and the prize tracks, teach it live, mentor all weekend, judge.

Also: Move review of partner contracts, on-call support for exchanges and DEXs, a hands-on DeepBook course, video tutorials for the Walrus Foundation, and Builder Growth Champions: a zero-budget builder program designed on paper and cancelled in a reorganisation before its first cohort.
